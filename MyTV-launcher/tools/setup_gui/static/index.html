Preparing Your TV for One-Time Installation

Before your AI agent installs MyTV over Wi-Fi, turn on Developer Debugging on your TV using the matching card below (takes about 60 seconds).

Local Computer Readiness

Don't worry if something shows Missing — your AI agent can install JDK 17 or Android platform-tools automatically.

Google TV & Android TV (Sony, TCL, Xiaomi, Philips, Hisense, onn)

  1. Open your TV's Settings → System → About.
  2. Scroll down to Android TV OS Build and press OK 7 times until it says "You are now a developer!".
  3. Go back one screen to System → Developer options and turn ON USB debugging (and Network debugging if listed).
  4. Find your TV's Wi-Fi IP under Settings → Network & Internet → your Wi-Fi name.

Chromecast with Google TV / Android 14 Streamers

  1. Enable Developer options as shown on the left.
  2. On Android 14, open Developer options → Wireless debugging and turn it ON.
  3. Choose Pair device with pairing code — give the 6-digit code and IP:port shown on screen to your AI agent.
  4. Your AI agent will pair and open port 5555 automatically.

LG webOS Smart TVs

  1. Install the official Developer Mode app from the LG Content Store and sign in with a free LG Developer account.
  2. Turn Dev Mode Status ON (your TV will restart).
  3. Re-open Developer Mode and turn Key Server ON, then share the IP and 6-character passphrase when prompted.

Samsung Tizen Smart TVs & Amazon Fire TV

  • Samsung Tizen: Open the Apps panel, press 1 2 3 4 5 on your remote, switch Developer mode ON, enter this computer's IP, and restart the TV.
  • Amazon Fire TV: Open Settings → My Fire TV → About, click your Fire TV name 7 times, then enable ADB Debugging under Developer Options.

Troubleshooting & Common Questions

Quick answers for manual setup, TV prompts, adding optional tabs later, and switching back to your stock TV launcher.

What steps do I have to do manually vs. what does the AI agent do?

What you do manually: (1) Enable Developer/Debugging mode on your TV once, (2) create your own free TMDB key (and any optional keys you want), (3) click "Allow Debugging" on your TV screen when the agent connects, and (4) if using Windows PC Gaming, install Sunshine and approve one Administrator PowerShell setup command.

What the AI agent handles automatically: Checking/installing build tools, configuring files, verifying your TV brand's protected system apps, building MyTV.apk + MyTV-Remote.apk, installing over Wi-Fi, and encrypting your keys into your TV's hardware vault.

What if my AI agent fails to set an API key — how do I enter keys myself?

You have two easy ways without needing the AI agent:

1. Directly on your TV: Open MyTV → Settings → Get Started (Initial Setup & Cloud). You can pair Trakt via QR code, sign in to Stremio, scan Wi-Fi for WiZ bulbs, and enter API keys right on screen.

2. In this Setup Studio: Open the API Keys & Save tab above, paste your keys, click Save Settings Locally, and ask your agent to rebuild/sync — or run python MyTV-launcher/tools/send_key.py <TV_IP> --sync-config.

I skipped Gaming or Smart Home during setup. Can I add those tabs later?

Yes — both features are built in and modular! You can turn either tab on at any time:

On your TV: Go to Settings → Home & Appearance → Interface & Units and change Gaming Tab Visibility or Smart Home Tab Visibility to Always Show or Automatic.

With your AI agent: Simply tell your agent "Enable the Gaming tab (or Smart Home tab) on my MyTV and guide me through the keys/PC setup" — it only needs to update enable_gaming=true or enable_smarthome=true and sync your keys.

Google Play Protect showed "Risky app blocked" or "Built for an older version of Android"

On Android 14 TVs, Play Protect shows this prompt the first time an app with a remote-button accessibility helper is installed over ADB. Select More details → Install anyway on your TV remote. Future updates won't ask again. Always install via ADB rather than a TV file-manager app so Play Protect doesn't grey out the option.

How do I return to my TV's original factory home screen?

MyTV never uninstalls or deletes your stock launcher. Open MyTV → Settings → System → Launcher Mode and select Original TV Home — your factory home screen and stock settings return immediately. Always switch back to Original before uninstalling MyTV.
